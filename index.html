SECURITY BOUNDARY

No false security.

SME Shield is intentionally narrow. It helps a business understand and act on practical protection gaps. It does not promise complete cybersecurity.

What SME Shield does

  • Helps identify important devices and accounts
  • Prioritizes practical actions
  • Helps verify backup readiness
  • Provides an incident-response path
  • Uses simulated AI recommendations in this prototype

What SME Shield does NOT do

  • Guarantee complete cybersecurity
  • Replace cybersecurity professionals
  • Monitor or score employees
  • Act as antivirus software
  • Automatically resolve every security incident
